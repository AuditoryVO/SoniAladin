SoniAladin

Exploring Aladin Sky Atlas through sound

Key Controls Summary:
  • Simplified Voice Control: Say "start", "pause", or "stop" (Requires microphone access)
  • Right Arrow: Start or continue sonification
  • Left Arrow (Press / Double): Pause or Stop & Reset
  • SHIFT + Right Arrow: Explore the object in SIMBAD
  • + / - Keys: ALADIN Zoom in or Zoom out
  • SHIFT + (+ / -): Increase or reduce low threshold
  • Up / Down Arrow: Choose top-wise or down-wise exploration
  • SHIFT + Control Key: Slow down exploration (-25%)
  • SHIFT + Command Key: Speed up exploration (+25%)
  • SHIFT + Option / Alt Key: Reset exploration speed (Default speed)
