SoniAladin

Exploring Aladin Sky Atlas through sound

Key Controls Summary:
  • Simplified Voice Control: Say "start", "pause", "stop", "horizontal", or "vertical"
  • Right Arrow: Start or continue right/down exploration
  • Left Arrow: Start or continue left/up exploration
  • Down Arrow (Press / Double): Pause or Stop & Reset
  • Up Arrow: Explore the object in SIMBAD
  • + / - Keys: ALADIN Zoom in or Zoom out
  • SHIFT + (+ / -): Increase or reduce low threshold
  • SHIFT + Control Key: Slow down exploration (-25%)
  • SHIFT + Command Key: Speed up exploration (+25%)
  • SHIFT + Option / Alt Key: Reset exploration speed
